Is Your Website Holding Your Business Back?

A redesign should change how customers move from first visit to enquiry, not only how your site looks. We rebuild outdated business websites around trust, mobile use and a clear next step.

Get a Digital Presence Assessment Request a Quote
SIGNS IT'S TIME

Common signs a website needs work.

It looks dated

Mismatched styles and old photos make a good business look unreliable.

It's awkward on a phone

Tiny text, hard buttons or sideways scrolling send visitors away.

It's unclear what you offer

If visitors can't tell what you do in a few seconds, they leave.

Contact is hard

Details are buried, forms are long, or there is no easy way to message you.

Little reason to trust you

No real work, no clear business details, nothing that proves you are who you say.

It's slow or broken

Pages that load slowly or links that fail cost you enquiries.

WHAT WE CHANGE

The customer journey, not just the colours.

We look at how a visitor moves from arriving to enquiring, then rebuild each step.

Mobile experience

Designed for phones first, then scaled up for larger screens.

Enquiry journey

Quote forms, WhatsApp and booking placed where customers look for them.

Messaging and structure

Clear services, plain language and pages that follow how customers decide.

Search foundations

Sensible titles, headings, page structure and links, aligned with your Google Business Profile.

We don't promise that a redesign will raise your rankings or bring a set number of enquiries. It removes the obstacles we can see and gives your site a stronger base to work from. If you aren't sure where the problem is, our guide on why a website may not generate enquiries is a good place to start.

BEFORE AND AFTER

What changes in practice.

Weak contact journey → simple enquiry journey

A visitor can ask, message or book without hunting for details.

Outdated mobile experience → mobile-first design

The site works properly on the device most people use.

Vague services → clear service structure

Each service is easy to find, understand and act on.

PROCESS

How a redesign works.

1. Review

We look at your current site, how customers reach it and where they drop off.

2. Plan

We agree the pages, message and enquiry paths before any design.

3. Rebuild

We design and build the new site, keeping what works.

4. Launch and look after

We check titles, links and redirects, then offer ongoing maintenance.

QUESTIONS

Website redesign FAQs.

Will I lose my Google visibility if I redesign?

It can happen if a redesign is done carelessly. We keep useful pages and addresses where we can, redirect any that change and check titles and headings before launch. No one can guarantee outcomes, but the risk can be managed.

Do you need to rebuild everything?

Not always. In the review we tell you honestly whether to improve what you have or rebuild it.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content is ready. We give a timeline with your quote.
