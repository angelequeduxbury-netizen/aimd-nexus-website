Questions

Google Business Profile FAQs.

Will this get me to the top of Maps?

We can't promise that. We make your profile accurate and complete so you compete well for relevant searches.

Do you need access to my profile?

We usually need manager access, which you control and can remove at any time.

How often should it be updated?

Whenever hours, services or offers change, and with fresh photos and posts from time to time.

Do you write or buy reviews?

No. We help you ask genuine customers for honest reviews and respond well to the ones you receive.
