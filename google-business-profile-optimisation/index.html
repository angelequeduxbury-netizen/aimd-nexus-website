Google Business Profile Optimisation for Local Businesses

Your profile is often the first thing a customer sees. We set it up to be accurate, complete and connected to your website, so more views can become calls, messages and visits.

Get a Digital Presence Assessment Request a Quote
MORE THAN A LISTING

A discovery and a conversion point.

A Google Business Profile shows on Search and Maps. It can answer a customer's questions before they ever visit your site, which makes it part of your digital foundation, not a standalone trick. It works best alongside a clear website and sound local SEO.

WHAT A STRONG PROFILE CONTAINS

The essentials.

Accurate business information

Name, address or service area, phone, hours and website.

The right categories

A primary category that fits, with relevant secondary ones.

Services and a clear description

What you do, written for customers.

Real photos

Your work, premises and team.

Posts and updates

Offers, news and changes in hours.

Customer actions

Call, message, directions or booking links that work.

MISSED OPPORTUNITIES

What we often see.

Common gaps include a category that is too generic, out of date hours, no services listed, few photos, unanswered reviews, and a website link that points to a general page instead of the most relevant one.

PROCESS

How we optimise it.

1. Review

We check your profile and how it matches your website.

2. Correct

We fix information, categories and services.

3. Complete

We add photos, description and customer actions.

4. Maintain

We help you keep it current and respond to reviews.

PROFILE AND WEBSITE

They should say the same thing.

Customers move between your profile and your site, so details and wording should match, and the profile should link to the page that best fits the search. We never write, buy or incentivise reviews, and we can't guarantee Maps placement or enquiries. We help you ask genuine customers and respond well.

QUESTIONS

Google Business Profile FAQs.

Will this get me to the top of Maps?

We can't promise that. We make your profile accurate and complete so you compete well for relevant searches.

Do you need access to my profile?

We usually need manager access, which you control and can remove at any time.

How often should it be updated?

Whenever hours, services or offers change, and with fresh photos and posts from time to time.
