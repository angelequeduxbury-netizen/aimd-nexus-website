I Need a Website. Where Do I Start?

AiMD Nexus · Published 8 October 2026

Start with what you want visitors to do, not with how the site should look. Once that is clear, the pages, wording and tools mostly follow.

Decide what the website is for

Pick the main action: a quote request, a booking, a phone call or a purchase. One clear goal makes every other decision easier, and your site should lead people to it from every page.

Know who you are talking to

Think about where your customers are and what they search for. A business serving Gqeberha (Port Elizabeth) and nearby towns should say so in plain words, because that is how customers and Google understand where you work.

Gather the basics before you build

  • A short, honest description of each service
  • Photos of your real work and premises
  • Contact details and opening hours
  • The areas you serve
  • Anything that builds trust, such as memberships, qualifications or reviews you have permission to use

Choose a small set of pages

Most small businesses need a home page, a page for each main service, an about page and a contact page. Add online booking if customers regularly ask to book.

Plan to be found

A good website still needs people to reach it. Set up your Google Business Profile and keep your details consistent. Our pages on local SEO and Google Business Profile optimisation explain how those fit together.
