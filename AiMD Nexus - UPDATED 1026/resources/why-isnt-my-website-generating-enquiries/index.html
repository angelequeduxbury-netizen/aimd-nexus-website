Why Isn't My Website Generating Enquiries?

AiMD Nexus · Published 8 October 2026

Either the wrong people are reaching it, or the right people arrive and can't see a reason to trust you or an easy way to get in touch. Work out which before you change anything.

Check whether people are arriving

If very few people visit, the problem is visibility and you should look at local SEO. If people visit but don't enquire, the problem is on the page.

Common reasons visitors leave

  • It isn't clear what you do in the first screen
  • The site is hard to use on a phone
  • Contact details are buried or the form is too long
  • There is nothing that shows you are credible
  • The page is slow
  • Too many choices and no clear next step

Make the next step obvious

Pick one main action and repeat it where visitors need it. For many local businesses, a WhatsApp button next to a form works well. See should your business website have WhatsApp.

Fix things in the right order

  • Message: say clearly what you do
  • Contact path: make it quick
  • Trust: add real proof
  • Speed and mobile use
  • Then visibility

When a redesign makes sense

If the structure itself is the problem, patching rarely helps. A website redesign rebuilds the journey, and small business websites covers a fresh build.
