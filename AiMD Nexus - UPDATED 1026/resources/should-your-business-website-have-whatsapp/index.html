Should Your Business Website Have WhatsApp?

AiMD Nexus · Published 8 October 2026

For most local service businesses, yes. WhatsApp is widely used in South Africa and many customers find a message easier than a form or a call. It works best alongside a form or phone number, not instead of them.

Why it helps

It is familiar, quick on a phone and lets customers send photos for a quote. That lowers the effort of making contact.

When it may not suit

If you need formal records, can't answer messages promptly or handle sensitive personal information, a form or phone call may be better. Take care with customers' personal information in any channel.

How to add it well

  • Use a click-to-chat button with a short pre-filled message
  • Use a business number you can manage, ideally the WhatsApp Business app
  • Say when you reply
  • Keep a form or phone option too

What to avoid

Don't hide the button, use a number nobody monitors, or leave messages unanswered. A slow reply can cost more than having no button.

Where it fits

WhatsApp is one part of an enquiry path. We build it into small business websites alongside forms and booking.
