GQEBERHA OR PORT ELIZABETH?

One city, two names customers still use.

The city's official name is Gqeberha, but many customers still search for Port Elizabeth, and plenty of local businesses are listed under both. A good website uses the words your customers actually use, so we write for how people really search instead of forcing one name everywhere.
