WHAT IT SHOULD DO

Six jobs your website has to do.

1. Be found

Show up when people search for what you do, in your area.

2. Explain clearly

Say what you offer and who it's for in plain words.

3. Build trust

Show real work, real contact details and a professional first impression.

4. Make contact easy

A visible phone number, form or WhatsApp button.

5. Capture the enquiry

Turn interest into a quote request, booking or sale.

6. Work on mobile

Load quickly and read well on a phone.
